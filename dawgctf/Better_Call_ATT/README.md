## Solution

The key landmark in the image is the **AT&T logo** visible on a building behind the parking garage, combined with the surrounding downtown skyline.

**Step 1 - Identify the filming location.**  
*Better Call Saul* is primarily filmed in Albuquerque, New Mexico. A reverse image search on the frame confirmed the visual match to downtown Albuquerque.

**Step 2 - Narrow down the garage.**  
Cross-referencing known Better Call Saul filming locations and the AT&T building in the background, the garage was identified as the **Albuquerque Civic Plaza Parking Garage** on Marquette Ave NW.

**Step 3 - Find the phone number.**  
Searching for the garage on Google Maps / business directories returned the phone number: **1-505-768-4575**.
